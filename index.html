What is Neo QS?

Neo QS is an AI-powered quantity surveying platform designed to automate takeoffs, Bills of Quantities (BOQs), cost estimation, and quantity surveying spreadsheets. It also provides an intelligent AI assistant for project-specific guidance and integrates international measurement standards into a modern digital workflow.

Professional features will be available only to licensed/registered Quantity Surveyors to help maintain professional standards.
